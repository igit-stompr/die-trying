Die Trying

Not the best at Wardogs but still play for the win? Looking for a chill crew to hang out with that doesn't throw tantrums, use slurs, or act like children? Consider joining DT.

Who We Are:

  • We are a tight, teamwork-driven fireteam built for mature gamers who want to play the objective without the baggage of mega-clan noise or public lobby chaos.
  • We aim for 3-5 man squad but it could get bigger if more of us are online. We just don't intend this clan to grow to a big size.
  • We Play for the Win: We aren't esports pros and we fully accept that we aren't the best on the field—but we play hard, coordinate, and care about the objective every single match.
  • We Go Rogue: When the wider team faction is throwing or completely hopeless, we don't quit. We break off into a rogue guerrilla unit to hit back-lines, run logistics, and manufacture our own fun.
  • Comms Discipline: We are adults looking to unwind. Our voice channels are strictly preserved for calm tactical info and relaxed banter. We have zero tolerance for tantrums, slurs, or childish antics. (We curse, we aren't prudes—we just like to keep it chill).
  • We care about this culture fit, and if it's not right because you're particularly frustrated and sweatier than the rest of us, we'll ask you to move on. If you don't think you're juvenile but we do, we'll ask you to move on... We want everyone who stays in the clan to be happy with everyone else in it. There are more places that fit those personalities than these in the FPS PVP world. :)
